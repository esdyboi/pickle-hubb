Play well

Court rules & guidelines.

Friendly reminders keep the facility safe, clean, and fun.

◒

Come ready

Wear sports attire and non-marking shoes. Bare feet, slippers, and sandals stay off court.

⌁

Keep it safe

Keep bags clear, follow staff guidance, and take care entering and exiting.

♢

Respect the gear

Return rentals after your session and report any damage promptly.

✦

Respect the game

Harassment, fighting, aggressive behavior, and disruptive language are not welcome.

◷

Arrive 10–15 minutes early and use only your reserved court.

♡

Play within your abilities and report injuries or unsafe conditions.
